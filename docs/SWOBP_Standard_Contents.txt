CONTENTS

A) SPECIAL CHARACTERS
---------------------

Mark I Assault Droid.
Droideka Destroyer Droid.

B) ABILITIES
------------

Health.
Armor.
Power.
Resistance.
Agility.
Strength.

C) FORCE POWERS
---------------

Basic Powers (Jump, Speed, See).
Kinetic Powers (Push, Explode, Pull, Implode).
Lightsaber Skills (Fast, Medium, Strong, Fast-Medium, Medium-Strong, Dual, Staff, Defend, Throw).

D) OTHER FORCE POWERS
---------------------

Miscellaneous Powers (Heal, Regeneration).
Telekinetic Powers (Grip, Grasp).
Status Powers (Protect, Dark Aura, Absorb, Deadly Sight).
Energy Powers (Lightning, Judgement, Drain, Sever).
Influence Powers (Confusion, Corruption, Stasis, Insanity).
Other Status & Energy Powers (Rage, Valor, Destruction, Blinding).

E) LIGHTSABERS
--------------

Single, dual and staff sabers.
Blue, green, yellow, purple, orange, cyan, red, white, black, multicolor, and RGB colors.
Many saber hilts, including exotic hilts such as Grievous saber hilts and single saberstaff.

F) LIGHT WEAPONS
----------------

Other Pistols/Gauntlets (K-16, S5-H, SC-10, B-B, B-I, B-R).
Blaster Pistols (DL-44, DH-17, DC-17, W-34, G-44, SE-44C).
Blaster Rifles (E-11, A280, DC-15S, E-5B, EL-16, F-11D).
Blaster Carbines (BC, EE-3, BC-W, CO, BC-M, ST-C).
Ion/Flame/Ice Carbines (DEMP2, DC-24, DC-17P, CR-25).
Batons/Other Melee (Baton).

G) HEAVY WEAPONS
----------------

Sniper Rifles (DXR-6, DLT-20A, DC-15X, E-5S, Cycler, PSG).
Repeater Rifles (TL-50, DLT-18, DC-15A, T-21, DC-17M, FWMB-10).
Shot/Array Guns (FC-1, K-16S, DBA, ACP, CR-1, S-22).
Conc/Rotary Cannons (Stouker, LJ-70, Pulse, Sonic, Z-6, Z-12).
Rocket/Grenade Launchers (PLX-2M, MML, PLX-1, E-60R, MG-T, MG-F).

H) EXPLOSIVES
-------------

Grenades (Thermal, Flame, Dioxis, Ion, Sonic, Flash).
Mines (Thermal, Flame, Dioxis, Ion, Sonic, Flash).
Bombs (Thermal, Flame, Dioxis, Ion, Sonic, Flash).

I) ITEMS
--------

Standard Items (Bacta Canister, Shield Booster, TD2.3 Electrobinoculars, Grapple Hook).
Advanced Items (Force Field Projector, Training Remote, Sentry Turret, E-Web Blaster Cannon).
Standard & Advanced Throwers (Flame, Dioxis, Ice, Electro, Beam, Orbital).
Status Change Items (Cloak Generator, Shield Generator, Overload).

J) OTHER ITEMS
--------------

Jetpacks (Z-6M, Z-6B, Back, JT-12M, JT-12W, CT-20, RT-SB, RT-SW, RB).
Backpack Rockets (Thermal, Flame, Concussion).
Squad Teams (Empire, Rebellion, Mercenaries, Confederacy, Republic, Mandalorian, Sith Empire, Old Republic, Civilians).

K) VEHICLES
-----------

Vehicle Mount (74-Z, Tauntaun, Swoop-MP, AT-ST, Landspeeder, Rancor, AT-AT, Snowspeeder, Skiff, TIE Fighter, X-Wing, TIE Interceptor, TIE Bomber, Y-Wing, A-Wing, T-4A, YT-1300, Firespray-31, STAP, Eopie, FC-20, AAT, AT-PT, Bantha, Hailfire, AT-TE, N-1, Vulture, ARC-170, Tri-Fighter, 24SB, LAAT, V-Wing, STHPD, Ravensclaw, NHS).

L) BOTS
-------

Classes setup including Assault, Support, Officer, Specialist, Aerial, Gunner, Infiltrator, Hunter, Enforcer, Demolition, Grenadier, Incinerator, Pestifier, Freezer, Warrior, Jedi Knight, Jedi Master, Sith Assassin, and Sith Lord.

Bot Types setup including Battlefront (Gunner Behavior), Force Unleashed (Duelist Behavior), and Jedi Academy (Default Behavior).

M) NPCS
-------

Inspired from single-player for the cooperative gametype.

N) CLASSES
----------

Fully customizable free class in all gametypes except in the Holocron Free for All (only force class) and Siege (fixed classes) gametypes.

O) GAMETYPES
------------

Cooperative/Single Player.
Free for All.
Jedi Master.
Holocron.
Duel.
Power Duel.
Team Free for All.
Capture the Flag.
Capture the Ysalamiri.
Siege.

P) MAPS
-------

All JK2 and JKA maps.

Q) TEXTURES
-----------

Higher definition textures.
